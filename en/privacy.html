Photo Retro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

"Photo Retro" (the "app") is an Android app made by behappybill, an individual developer (the "developer"). This policy explains how the app handles personal information.

1. Summary

  • The app sends no data to the developer's servers or anyone else's. The app has no internet permission.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, ads, location data or face recognition.
  • Your photos and records stay on your device until you delete them.

3. Permissions

  • Photos: you can allow all photos, allow selected photos only, or pick photos one at a time with the system photo picker without any permission. You can change this in your device settings at any time.
  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on the reminder. Notifications contain no photos or notes.
  • Restart detection: used only to reschedule the reminder after the device restarts.
  • The app does not request location permission.

4. Exporting records

When you use "Export records", the app saves a ZIP file with your records and kept photos to a location you choose. The file contains your notes. If you save it to a service such as a cloud drive, that service's policy applies from then on. The app does not send the file anywhere else.

5. Backups

App data is excluded from your device's automatic backup. To move to a new phone, use "Export records" and "Import records".

6. Retention and deletion

Information stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a day's record in the app removes that record and its kept photos; uninstalling the app removes all data. Original photos in your gallery are never deleted.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 16 and over.

8. Your rights

The developer holds none of your personal information, so you can access, correct, delete and move it yourself on your device (edit or delete records, export records, uninstall the app).

  • Republic of Korea (Personal Information Protection Act): the person responsible for personal information is the developer, Seongjin Han, reachable at the email below.
  • EEA and UK (GDPR): the developer does not collect or process your personal data. You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.
  • United States (California and other state laws): the developer does not sell or share personal information.

9. Changes

If a new feature such as cloud backup changes how information is processed, we will announce it in the app and on this page before it takes effect.
